About Me

Hello! I’m Amelie Coetzee, a dedicated graphic design student at NGI with a passion for creativity and innovation. My journey in the world of design spans branding, photography, and web development, allowing me to explore various avenues of artistic expression.

With a keen eye for detail and a love for storytelling, I believe that design is more than just aesthetics; it’s about creating meaningful connections. Through my work in branding, I strive to help businesses communicate their unique identities and values effectively. My photography captures the beauty of everyday moments, allowing me to express my perspective on the world.

In addition to my design pursuits, I enjoy honing my skills in web development, where I can combine functionality and creativity to craft engaging online experiences. As I continue studying interactive visual communication and branding, I am excited to grow as a designer and contribute to projects that inspire and uplift.

I am always eager to learn, collaborate, and push the boundaries of my creativity. Let’s connect and explore the possibilities together!
